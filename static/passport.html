🕊️ Portable Credential Guarantee (UN SDG 8)

Under United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 8, worker skill credentials and verified accident-free duty records are owned entirely by the worker. They can present this verifiable link to any future employer or site contractor to secure fair wages without re-testing or credential exploitation.
